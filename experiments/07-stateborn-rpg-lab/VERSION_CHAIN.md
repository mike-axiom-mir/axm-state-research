## Knowledge progression

1. Deterministic state and receipts were possible, but naive global copying was
   a measurable failure.
2. Sparse recovery made logical extent cheap enough for bounded relational
   experiments.
3. Separating policy input from consequence made “curiosity incentive” testable
   without pretending the machine valued success.
4. Separating proposer seats from referee authority made coexistence testable
   without pretending a stand-in was a connected AI.
5. Separating source ownership from session projection made a local
   “state crossing” testable without pretending a person moved.
6. Separating typed machine payloads from observer interpretation made bounded
   coordination testable without pretending machines invented a language.

Failures, fixtures, and exclusions remain part of the result.
