authored collision fixture for testing the intersection rules. Genesis creates
no event and no adventure thread. A `three_way_mark` appears only after all
three roles commit recorded signal events at a shared cell.

## Honest result

The mechanism makes unequal kinds of agency coexist without giving any seat
silent authority over canonical state. Twelve deterministic 64-cycle probes
all replayed, kept the machine policy boundary clean, produced mixed growth
and damage, and formed three-role marks. The amount and quality of adventure
remain unproven, and the offline stand-in is not an AI model.
