AXM STATEBORN RPG LAB v0.7.0
================================

WHAT THIS IS
------------
A local bottom-up state research chain with seven separately preserved steps.
The new Hostile Transport Microscope tests whether the exact v0.6 packets keep
their state, consent, recovery, and failure boundaries under bad delivery.

OPEN THE NEWEST TEST
--------------------
1. Open the "dist" folder.
2. Double-click "transport.html".
3. Choose one of the ten frozen held-out routes.
4. Step one transport tick at a time or run the selected route.
5. Inspect its in-flight envelopes and transport event ledger.
6. Watch retry, duplicate suppression, expiry, and checkpoint recovery.
7. Compare solved, refused, state-deadlocked, and transport-deadlocked routes.
8. Run the complete hostile-transport gate.

No account, internet, AI connection, installation, server, or real personal
data is required.

SEVEN KNOWLEDGE STEPS
---------------------
v0.1 canonical receipts: deterministic, but whole-world copy/hash scaled badly.
v0.2 sparse relations: sleeping logical cells plus causal autonomous reactions.
v0.3 curiosity boundary: novelty input separated from observer consequences.
v0.4 coexistence boundary: human/machine/AI-compatible proposals, one referee.
v0.5 ownership boundary: consented projections compose, return, and separate.
v0.6 protocol boundary: typed packets coordinate, refuse, or deadlock honestly.
v0.7 transport boundary: hostile delivery recovers or fails with typed evidence.

All seven named ZIP snapshots are retained. A later version does not rewrite an
earlier failure, fixture, limitation, or result.

WHAT TO WATCH
-------------
- Dropped packets retry within an explicit attempt limit.
- Duplicate and expired deliveries have no state effect.
- Delayed and reordered packets remain visible in the ledger.
- Disconnect recovery reconstructs from a digest-bound checkpoint.
- Corruption refuses before a later clean retry succeeds.
- Consent refusal and insufficient state remain protocol outcomes.
- Repeated packet loss remains an honest retained transport deadlock.
- Every accepted machine payload remains state-only and replayable.
