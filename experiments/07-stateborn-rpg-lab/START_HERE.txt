AXM STATEBORN RPG LAB v0.5.0
================================

WHAT THIS IS
------------
A local bottom-up state research chain with five separately preserved steps.
The new Actor Capsule Crossing composes consented projections from two
independently owned fictional sources. It never claims to move a person.

OPEN THE NEWEST TEST
--------------------
1. Open the "dist" folder.
2. Double-click "capsules.html".
3. Inspect what Aster and Briar keep private.
4. Compose both allowlisted projections into separate session namespaces.
5. Select each projection and leave matching signals on the same cell.
6. Build proposed return packets.
7. Accept selected paths or refuse all; nothing writes back automatically.
8. Detach either projection and verify exact replay.
9. Use "Run crossing proof" for the complete bounded sequence.

No account, internet, AI connection, installation, server, or real personal
data is required.

FIVE KNOWLEDGE STEPS
--------------------
v0.1 canonical receipts: deterministic, but whole-world copy/hash scaled badly.
v0.2 sparse relations: sleeping logical cells plus causal autonomous reactions.
v0.3 curiosity boundary: novelty input separated from observer consequences.
v0.4 coexistence boundary: human/machine/AI-compatible proposals, one referee.
v0.5 ownership boundary: consented projections compose, return, and separate.

All five named ZIP snapshots are retained. A later version does not rewrite an
earlier failure, fixture, limitation, or result.

WHAT TO WATCH
-------------
- Private notes and recovery tokens never appear in capsules.
- Aster and Briar remain under different owner namespaces.
- Session actions cannot mutate either source.
- A shared signal appears only after matching independent event receipts.
- A return packet is a proposal, not write authority.
- Each owner chooses its accepted fields independently.
- Forged or stale packets fail closed.
- Detachment preserves evidence and both sources remain recoverable.
